How these are built. Every number comes from 2026 regular-season Statcast pitches (data through 2026-09-27) plus FanGraphs season lines. Grades are league percentiles mapped to the 20–80 scale (50 = average, 10 points = 1 SD) within qualified pools; Stuff is a pitch-shape model of expected count-neutral run value. The bottom-line bullets are generated by a rules library that ranks findings by notability — each sentence is a template filled with the computed number it cites. Count plans, attack plans and usage suggestions come from this repo's situational, location and arsenal models; their effects are small and are presented as direction, not prescription.
